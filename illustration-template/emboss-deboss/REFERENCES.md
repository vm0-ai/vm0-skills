## Provenance of the design direction

The user supplied [Adrian Punk's public post about emboss and deboss
posters](https://x.com/AdrianPunk115/status/2095082810172473824). Its general
print-design approach informed this independently written prompt resource:
paper texture, shallow relief, directional lighting, economical typography, and
theme-driven visual metaphors. The source post's images were inspected during
research and are not included in this package or used as image-generation
inputs. The author's prompt text is not reproduced in the package.
